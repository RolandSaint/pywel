## M1 source disposition

The retained manifest defines the exact dataset. M1 retains 77 evidence records: 49 CrimsonWiki records and 28 official-publication records. Historical private development records are outside that candidate unless explicitly retained.

| Baseline evidence cohort | M1 disposition |
| --- | --- |
| 267 unknown-license records retaining normalized facts | Excluded, with affected unsupported claims. Unknown rights are not repaired by relabeling. |
| 43 private observation artifact records | Excluded. Private research, preferences, and gameplay state are outside the public corpus. |
| 107 Fandom records labeled CC BY-SA 3.0 | Excluded from the finite candidate. Historical metadata records the license, but the current source license could not be independently retrieved. This is a scope decision, not a finding of infringement. |
| 49 CrimsonWiki records labeled CC BY-SA 4.0 | Retained with attribution and public provenance. Publisher terms were checked; that does not independently verify every game claim. |
| 28 official-publication records | Retained as attributed locators and compact publisher-intent assertions under the stated distribution boundary. No source bodies, assets, or publisher-IP grant. |
| 293 unknown-license locator-only records | Excluded after dependent claims were removed; no unused leads are retained. A locator did not clear the substantive normalized gameplay claims. |

Excluded material may return only through a reviewed contribution with a documented source and rights basis. Restoring a historical import packet is not a shortcut.

The source-copy review also excludes the historical patch-change text corpus, its completeness reviews, verbatim patch-bullet claims, and dependent verification strategies. Calling copied publisher prose a normalized summary does not resolve its rights. The retained patch records describe a finite selection of facts or source identity only; they do not claim complete patch-note ingestion.

## Attribution and redistribution

Keep this notice, LICENSE-DATA, third-party data notices, and relevant evidence records with redistributed datasets. Evidence supplies source titles, publishers, URLs, capture context, and normalization locators; notices supply licenses, creator-history links, and modification notices. Downstream adaptations must identify their changes and follow the applicable CC BY-SA terms.

A URL or hash is an integrity or location aid, not permission, proof of ownership, or independent verification. Do not ship source bodies, dialogue, long excerpts, media, maps, game files, private artifacts, or copied databases.

Read [SOURCE_POLICY](docs/SOURCE_POLICY.md) and [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md) for acceptance and correction/removal procedures. Automated checks confirm their configured boundary; they are not legal certification or final release readiness.
